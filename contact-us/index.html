How to Get to Koh Mak

Koh Mak Island is located in the Gulf of Thailand, 350 km from Bangkok and 60 km from Trat.

The best way to reach Koh Mak, is to go to the pier in Trat (Laem Ngop pier). Then take a one-hour speed boat to the island.

Contact us for the transfer from the pier.
